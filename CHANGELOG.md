# Changelog

All notable changes to Pixel TD. Each pull request adds a line under Unreleased; a release moves
them under its version (see CLAUDE.md, "Releasing").

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Project set up: CI (check, unit tests, Linux + Windows test builds), tag releases, issue and pull request templates.
- Engine skeleton: SDL3 window with a pixel-perfect 640x360 view (32 px tiles, whole-number scaling), fixed 60 Hz simulation clock, PCG32 seeded RNG, doctest unit tests.
- README and CLAUDE.md link the project board and the workflow page (#1).
- Design docs for review: game overview, world layout and map generator, tower system (#3).
- Map generator: a seeded 3840x2160 heightmap drawn seamless (brightness = height, hue = material) with mesas, cliff faces and towering rock formations; the core placed by the seed in the central third and 3 seeded spawns (edge or inside, opening at waves 1, 10, 20); camera with whole-number pan and zoom; developer seed tools (`--seed`, F5, `--map-shot`) (#5).
- Line of sight and range over the height field (terrain blocks direct fire and sight, rock formations block every shot, lobbed shots arc over terrain; range grows with height above the target); explored-ground fog that hides spawns until found; developer probes (P, `--probe X,Y`) with a reach-and-sight preview (#7).
- Developer overlays (O / Shift+O, `--overlay NAME`): elevation as colour bands with contour lines, and each of the generator's noise layers (three height octaves, combined height, mesa mask, formation roughness, material) in greyscale (#13).
- The map is 5120x2880 (2x2 1440p screens) on 16 px cells, with towers and the core snapping to an 8 px placement subgrid (sizes unchanged); zooming out continues past 1x to 1/2 and the whole map, drawn smooth; F hides and shows the fog (developer tool), and the fog is now its own layer, so lifting it no longer re-bakes the terrain (#15).
- Tuning: the downhill range bonus has no cap (it grows with the drop, +5% per 10 height units); mesas terrace every 20 height units instead of 40; the elevation overlay has a colour stop every 20 units (#19).
- Smoother mesas (terracing fades in over 8 cells from a mesa's edge, and table tops keep up to 6 units of the slope under them, so they stay buildable); every noise layer gets tunable octaves (count, size, lacunarity, persistence), curve, domain warp and shape; generator presets as text (settings only) (#21).
- Map-tuning panel (Tab, developer tool, Dear ImGui 1.93.0): seed, every generator and noise setting with a live low-detail preview while dragging, live stats, and named presets (settings only); `--preset FILE` and `--panel` (#23).
- The map is 3 x 3 bigger: 15360x8640 (960 x 540 cells), drawn straight from a cell-sized texture stretched by the GPU, so the game uses less than half the memory it did at a ninth of the size; zooming out halves down to the whole map; the elevation overlay draws contour lines as lines; spawns sit farther from the core (360 / 180, start values) and formations keep their density (54-108); map shots save at reduced scale (`--map-scale`) (#25).
- Tower range follows the grade between tower and target (the fall or rise per cell), not the tower's altitude: flat ground at any height gives the base range, a decline stretches the reach and an incline shrinks it, up to +-50% both ways (start values); the tuning panel has a towers, sight and range section (#27).
- Enemy pathfinding and mazing, phase 1 core (#11, design/pathfinding.md): placeholder walkers route from the spawns to the core over a flow field on the 8 px subgrid (any 16 px gap is walkable, an 8 px gap isn't), by the fastest route (shallow water -35%, marsh -25%, mud -20%, trees -15% speed); light and heavy placeholder blocks shape the route; walkers sealed in break the cheapest block in their way; the core takes damage. Deterministic and integer-only, every number tunable (`SimParams`).
- Pathfinding phase 1 in the game (#11): B builds light and heavy placeholder blocks (right-click removes), E sends walkers (violet beetles, hidden in the fog) from every spawn and Shift+E streams them, R shows each spawn's route with its walk time (orange when sealed, with the block the walkers will break outlined); the HUD shows the core's health ("core fallen" at 0), the walkers and the last route rebuild time, and a hovered walker's speed, health and state; the tuning panel has an Enemies and blocks section; developer flags `--block`, `--heavy`, `--send`, `--stream`, `--routes`.
- The test arena, phase 2 core (#11, design/test-arena.md): placeholder light and heavy test towers that are blocks with a gun (range, damage, fire rate, shot speed and health, sight past rock) and shoot pooled projectiles that can miss, with first / closest / strongest / last targeting and per-tower damage, DPS and kills; waves (count, interval, spawns, health and speed scaling) with summaries; routes rebuilt on a worker thread and used a fixed number of ticks later (20, start), so placing a block no longer stalls the sim and runs stay repeatable; the live-tuning file format (every `SimParams` value) and saved layouts (seed, map settings, blocks and towers).
- The test arena in the game (#11 phase 2): T builds light and heavy test towers (teal, with a turret that turns to its target) that shoot visible shots; hovering or selecting a tower shows its reach and its DPS, damage, kills and targeting (G cycles it); Space pauses, `.` steps a tick, `[` and `]` change the speed (1/4 to 8x); the panel has a wave section with summaries, the test towers' numbers, the selected tower and named layouts; `tuning.txt` next to the game reloads when it changes; routes rebuild in the background (marked pending until they land); the HUD shows the time, the latest wave and performance; developer flags `--tower`, `--heavy-tower`, `--layout`, `--wave`, `--speed`, `--pause-at`, `--select`.

### Changed
- Faster CI: the check and unit-test jobs are merged into one core-only job (no SDL), and tools, apt packages and compiled objects are cached between runs; the unit tests only run when a push changes a system or object (#17).
- The map is flat (owner's decision): rock formations, now twice as many (108-216, cap 15%, tunable in the panel), are the only terrain obstacle. They block walking, building, sight and direct fire; lobbed shots arc over them; range is a plain circle. Heights, mesas, cliffs, range by grade, contours and the height overlays are removed, kept in `archive/heightmap/` with #29's simplex and erosion experiments. Generation takes about 55 ms (was about 140) (#30).
- The map is laid out like a colony-sim map (our own generator and placeholder look): mountains (about 20%, mostly near the edges) with caves, a river on most maps (deep middle, shallow banks, 2-4 fords) and 3-8 lakes, ground types (soil, rich soil, sand, gravel, mud, marsh, shallow and deep water) and forests. Rock blocks walking, building, sight and direct fire (lobbed shots arc over); deep water blocks walking and building; shallow water, mud, marsh and trees block building only. Every walkable cell is reachable. Simplex noise is back as a noise basis, sampled per octave on a grid matched to its feature size. Mountains replace the rock formations; the panel has Mountains, Caves, Water, Ground and Plants sections, and the overlays show mountain, fertility and moisture noise. Generation takes about 210 ms (#32).
- Trees are drawn as small generated sprites with shadows (placeholders until the style board) at 1/2 zoom and closer; further out the forests show as darker cells (#32).
- The view is an oblique 3/4 view (looks only; every rule stays on the flat grid): mountains show a cliff wall on their south faces (drawn over the open cell below the rock), trees stand on their cells, the core and spawns stand as boxes, and rivers and lakes sit below a bank lip. Developer option `--look X,Y` starts the camera on a cell (#35).
